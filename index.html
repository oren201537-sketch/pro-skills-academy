How To Use This

Videos. Tap any orange time chip or "Play drill" button. The drill plays in the player at the top, starts at the right second and stops by itself at the end of the drill. "Play whole course" runs every drill in the course one after another, like one long video for the topic.

Timestamps. Drill start and stop times come from each video's chapter markers on YouTube. They can be off by a few seconds. A video marked "full session" without a chapter list has no chapters, so it plays straight through.

Cues and doses. Coaching cues, common mistakes and level doses are a written summary that matches what each segment teaches. They are not word-for-word quotes from the coaches. When the video and these notes differ, follow the video.

Levels. Beginner means learning the technique with no defender. Intermediate means game speed with a passive defender or a pad. Pro means live reps, decisions and fatigue. Only move a player up a level once they can do the current level cleanly at game speed.
